### Seção 2 — A Solução (3 pilares)

**Título:** Três perguntas que nenhum outro app responde

**Pilar 1 — "Posso gastar isso agora?"**
> Abra o simulador, digite o valor. Em 1 segundo, veja o impacto no seu orçamento, nas suas próximas noites, no seu ritmo. Verde = tranquilo. Amarelo = atenção. Vermelho = para.

**Pilar 2 — "Quantas noites no bar eu ainda posso ter?"**
> O TripPilot não pensa em dias — pensa em *ocasiões*. Ele aprende quanto custa uma noite no bar, uma ida ao mercado, um almoço fora. E te diz: "3 noites no bar, 5 idas ao mercado, 1 passeio."

**Pilar 3 — "Quem me deve e quanto?"**
> Dividiu a conta? O TripPilot organiza, calcula quem deve quanto, e manda um link de cobrança pelo WhatsApp. Sem constrangimento. Sem planilha compartilhada.

## Parte 2 — Landing Page (EN)

### Hero

**Headline:**
> Know if you can order the next drink without blowing your trip budget?

**Sub-headline:**
> TripPilot answers in real time. Not a tracker that shows what you already spent — it's the honest friend that tells you if you can spend now.

**CTA:** Install free (Android) | Use in browser — no signup

---

### Problem

**Headline:** Ever finished a trip like this?

> "I overspent and only realized on the last day."
>
> Most travelers spend more than planned. Not because they don't care — because they didn't have the right info at the right moment. Spreadsheets show the past. TripPilot shows the future.

---

### Solution (3 pillars)

**Pillar 1 — "Can I spend this right now?"**
> Open the simulator. Type the amount. In 1 second, see the impact on your budget, your upcoming nights, your pace. Green = go. Yellow = careful. Red = stop.

**Pillar 2 — "How many bar nights can I still afford?"**
> TripPilot doesn't think in days — it thinks in *occasions*. It learns how much a bar night costs, a market run, a restaurant lunch. And tells you: "3 bar nights, 5 market runs, 1 outing left."

**Pillar 3 — "Who owes me and how much?"**
> Split a bill? TripPilot tracks it, calculates who owes what, and sends a charge link via WhatsApp. No awkwardness. No shared spreadsheet.

---

### Outing Mode

**Headline:** 11 PM at the bar. The app that goes with you.

> Start an outing and log each drink with one tap. TripPilot shows in real time:
> - How much you've spent tonight
> - How many drinks still fit the budget
> - Progressive alerts: "one more fits" → "last safe one" → "from here, it eats into other plans"
>
> It's not annoying. It's honest.
> And when you save? It says: **"You saved enough for an extra night out. Go for it!"**

---

### Features grid

| Feature | Line |
|---|---|
| Multi-currency | Spend in CZK, see in EUR. Auto-converted. |
| Fully offline | Works on the plane, in the mountains, no Wi-Fi. Always. |
| Spend map | See on a satellite map where every cent went. |
| AI receipt scan | Snap the receipt. The app reads and fills it in. |
| Smart piggy bank | Spent less today? The difference is saved for harder days. |
| Wise import | Import your CSV statement and the app sorts it all out. |
| No signup | No login. No email. No data collected. Your money, your device. |
| Secure backup | Export everything as JSON. Import on another phone. Nothing in the cloud. |

---

### Group Split

**Headline:** No more "we'll sort it out later"

> Create a group (the Eurotrip, the apartment, the BBQ), add people, log expenses. TripPilot calculates who owes what and generates a public link — anyone opens it, sees their part, marks as paid. No app needed.
>
> **Unlike Tricount:** here the split is part of your budget. The app knows that €15 dinner already left your "free to spend."

---

### Privacy

**Headline:** Zero data collected. For real.

> No login. No account. No analytics. No data sent to any server (except links YOU choose to share). Everything on your phone.

---

### CTA Final

**Headline:** Your next trip doesn't have to end with "I spent too much"

> Free. No signup. Install and start in 2 minutes.
