# TripPilot — Competitive Landscape

> Last updated: 2026-06-08

## Overview

TripPilot occupies a unique position: **real-time spending decision support during travel**. No competitor fully covers this space.

## Competitor Analysis

### TravelSpend (Primary Reference)
- **Platform**: Android, iOS (500K+ downloads on Google Play, updated June 2026)
- **What they do well**: Trip budget tracking, daily budget recalculation (remaining ÷ days left), surplus/deficit tracking, exclude special expenses from daily average, future expense reservations, multi-currency, expense sharing, offline support, CSV export
- **What they lack**: Outing mode, progressive alerts during spending, "can I spend?" simulator, occasion-based forecasting, three-limit system (ideal/safe/max), next-drink impact, scenario planner with trade-offs
- **Pricing**: Free with premium features
- **Key differentiator**: Best-in-class travel expense tracker
- **Our advantage over them**: We focus on the decision moment, not just recording. They tell you what happened; we tell you what will happen if you order another drink

### Daily Budget Original
- **Platform**: iOS (App Store)
- **What they do well**: Rolling daily budget, carry-over logic (saved days increase future budget), planned expense deductions
- **What they lack**: Travel-specific features, phases, shared expenses, outing mode
- **Pricing**: Paid app
- **Key differentiator**: Personal recurring budget management
- **Our advantage**: Travel-focused with phases, sharing, and occasion profiles

### Tricount
- **Platform**: Android, iOS, Web
- **What they do well**: Group expense splitting, who-owes-who, multiple currencies, bunq card auto-import
- **What they lack**: Budget management, daily limits, forecasting, spending guidance
- **Pricing**: Free with premium
- **Key differentiator**: Group expense splitting
- **Our advantage**: We combine splitting with budget intelligence

### Splitwise
- **Platform**: Android, iOS, Web
- **What they do well**: Debt tracking, group balances, simplified settlements, multiple currencies
- **What they lack**: Trip budget tracking, spending guidance, forecasting
- **Pricing**: Freemium
- **Key differentiator**: Persistent group debt management
- **Our advantage**: Budget-aware splitting, not just debt tracking

### Google Sheets / Notion (DIY)
- **What they do well**: Full customization, formulas, sharing
- **What they lack**: Mobile UX, quick-add, real-time alerts, offline, occasion learning
- **Our advantage**: Purpose-built UX for in-the-moment decisions

## Our Positioning

**"TripPilot helps you spend confidently when you can and stop when you should."**

We don't compete on expense recording (TravelSpend does that well). We compete on the moment of decision — the bar at 11 PM, the restaurant menu, the impulse buy. No app currently answers "how many bar nights can I still afford?" in real-time.
