## Analysis Framework

- **What they do well** (features, UX, pricing)
- **What they lack** (gaps, pain points users report)
- **Pricing model** (free/freemium/paid/subscription)
- **Target audience** (solo travelers, families, groups, digital nomads)
- **Key differentiator** (what makes them unique)
